## Factor list decisions

## Map data notes (Phase 2)

- **Boundaries (S39):** KOSTAT SGIS si-gun-gu boundaries, **2018 vintage**, filtered to SGIS province codes 24 (Gwangju), 35 (Jeollabuk-do) and 36 (Jeollanam-do): 42 features. Processed with mapshaper 0.6.102: islands under 0.5 km² removed, 10 % Visvalingam simplification with `keep-shapes` and planar, sliver cleanup, coordinate precision 1e-4°. Output: `data/honam-boundaries.geojson`, 454 KB. Province names follow the 2018 data (전라북도, 전라남도). Since then Jeollabuk-do has been renamed Jeonbuk State (전북특별자치도, 2024), and Gwangju and Jeollanam-do were merged into the Jeonnam–Gwangju Integrated Special City on 1 Jul 2026 (S31). The source data does not show reclaimed Saemangeum land; Saemangeum is shown as a point (S41) and lies inside the map extent.
- **Site outline (S40):** OSM way 444717696 (제1전투비행단, `military=airfield`). Its computed area is 6.01 km², which matches the 1.85 M pyeong (6.12 km²) airfield component in S4. The full 8.26 km² candidate-complex boundary has not been published.
- **Coordinates:** GeoJSON order [lon, lat], WGS 84. "Exact" means the coordinates of a mapped object (an OSM element or a Wikipedia article point). "Approximate" means an administrative centroid standing in for an unpublished site, a schematic connector, or a radius circle. Every approximate feature carries a note explaining the substitution.
- **Distances:** Straight-line (great-circle, haversine, R = 6371.0088 km) distances from the vertex mean of the site outline (126.8024° E, 35.1217° N). They are not route distances.
- **Unit conversion:** 1 pyeong = 400/121 m² ≈ 3.305785 m²; 2.5 M pyeong = 8.264 km².
